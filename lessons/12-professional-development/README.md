### The future belongs to AI-native designers

AI is not a threat, it is a new era. The risk is underestimating AI while overestimating your own
skills. Change, learn, and adapt with urgency, then help shape what comes next.

### Hands-on is the new default

From individual contributor to VP, staying close to the work is now the expectation. As AI speeds
up execution, the judgment to craft the right thing beats polish. Punk is coming back: authenticity
over glossy perfection.

AI also makes strong designers faster. Fewer cycles are wasted on concepts that do not move the
needle, discovery and delivery merge, and the real output is a working product shipped quickly.

### The teams are merging

The lines between design, product, and engineering are blurring, and the merge is accelerating.

| Shift | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| AI literacy | A baseline expectation now, not a bonus |
| Ship to production | Designers ship, with no handoff mindset |
| PMs prototype | Long specs out, fast iterations in |
| Forward deployed engineering | Designers embedded alongside engineers, building together |

Taste is the through-line. AI makes a product functional, taste makes it stand out.

### Two diverging worlds

Expectations for designers have split sharply by company, and the gap widens every month.

| Moving slow | Moving fast |
|-------------|-------------|
| Make screens pretty and present them in review | Think in systems, move into code, run research, and ship |
