## Overview

Assess the ethical questions raised by your concept and define how the product will learn from use
without treating every signal as permission to optimise. Update the prototype where necessary and
consider whether conversational interaction is appropriate for the experience.

## Objective

1. Apply the ethical assessment taught in Lesson 10 to the product concept.
2. Identify the most important issues involving bias, fairness, transparency, privacy, and consent.
3. Define a practical learning and iteration plan with clear guardrails.
4. Apply conversational AI principles when they are relevant to the concept.

## Steps

### Step 1: Assess the concept

* Identify the decisions the AI influences and the risks created by those decisions.
* Examine relevant bias, fairness, transparency, privacy, and consent concerns.
* Keep the assessment specific to this product and sector.

### Step 2: Define the learning plan

* Identify the usage and feedback signals that would inform improvement.
* Explain how those signals will be reviewed and interpreted.
* Define guardrails and the conditions that require human review or intervention.

### Step 3: Consider the interaction model

* Apply conversational principles if the product uses chat or voice.
* If it does not, explain why another interaction model better serves the user and task.
* Update the prototype to represent the current concept.
