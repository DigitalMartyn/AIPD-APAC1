## Overview

Combine the work from the course into a portfolio-ready case study. Present a coherent story from
the original problem through product definition, prototyping, testing, experience design,
responsibility, and future learning.

The case study must make sense to a reviewer encountering the concept for the first time.

## Objective

1. Build a clear end-to-end narrative from the capstone work.
2. Show evidence of your reasoning, decisions, and iteration.
3. Present the final experience and prototype professionally.
4. Reflect honestly on limitations and the next stage of the product.

## Deliverable

Your case study and presentation must include:

1. The problem, context, and target user.
2. The role of AI and the product proposition.
3. The workshop artefacts that materially shaped the concept.
4. The prototype and core experience flow.
5. Testing evidence and the iterations it informed.
6. Key interface states, including uncertainty and failure where relevant.
7. The ethical assessment and learning plan.
8. A definition of success, including measures and guardrails.
9. Limitations, future work, and personal reflection.
